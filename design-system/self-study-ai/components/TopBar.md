# TopBar

Sticky glass bar above the main column.

## Layout

`min-h-16`. Translucent card, blur, bottom border, soft shadow.

1. **Start** — mobile menu. Streak and points as round tinted chips.
2. **Center** — encouragement inside a muted pill. Rotates every ~5s.
3. **End** — sync chip, email when signed in, study CTA (primary Button). No Sign in button.

## Study CTA

- **Start studying** → `/study/`
- **Continue studying** when `localStorage` `self-study-active-session` is set.

## Encouragement phrases

- Small steps compound.
- One card at a time.
- Consistency beats intensity.
- Due cards wait for you.
- Keep the streak alive.
- Show up today.
