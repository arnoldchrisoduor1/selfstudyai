## Brand & Style
The design system translates focused cognitive workflow and study rigor into a deep midnight, low-phototaxic aesthetic. Conceived for sustained nocturnal deep work, technical mastery, and analytical discipline, it strips visual glare in favor of luminous hierarchy and architectural composure.

The aesthetic fuses **Technical Minimalism** with **Luminous Tactility**:
- **Void-first canvas:** Pure dark backgrounds paired with tiered slate-charcoal strata establish depth without muddy desaturation or optical strain.
- **Electric luminosity:** High-vibrancy indigo and violet accents pierce the low-light environment with purpose, signaling state changes, active workflows, and momentum via subtle atmospheric glows.
- **Micro-calibrated friction:** Hairline structural borders, crisp light text contrast, and compact data density emulate professional IDEs and precision engineering instruments.

## Colors
The palette abandons stark grayscale in favor of deep-tinted obsidian, midnight navy, and carbon slate. High-frequency interactions leverage spectral indigo and violet highlights that contrast against the void.

### Dark Mode Surfaces & Structural Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#0b0f19`):** Ground plane for application shells and outer framing.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#111827`):** Primary workspaces, sidebar decks, and base modular cards.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#1a2234`):** Elevated cards, input fills, nested tabs, and popover backdrops.
- **Surface Level 3 (`#1f293d`):** Hover states, contextual sheets, and overlay elements.
- **Surface Highlight (`#28344d`):** Active tool selections, code gutters, and focused row treatments.

### Outlines & Borders
- **Subtle Outline (`#1e293b`):** Passive structural lines, dividers, and background grid boundaries.
- **Interactive Outline (`#2d3748`):** Unfocused interactive bounds, card perimeters, and modular splits.
- **Accent Ring (`#6366f1` / `#818cf8`):** Active focus boundaries and highlighted milestones.

### Semantics & Status
Status indicators avoid heavy opaque fills. They render as translucent tinted backdrops bounded by hairline vector borders:
- **Success & Verified Retention:** Bright emerald (`#34d399`) over `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)` with `rgba(52, 211, 153, 0.25)` outline.
- **Warning & Streaks:** Amber flame (`#fbbf24`) over `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)` with `rgba(251, 191, 36, 0.25)` outline.
- **Alert & Critical Error:** Crimson spark (`#f87171`) over `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)` with `rgba(248, 113, 113, 0.25)` outline.

## Typography
Typography ensures effortless optical parsing against pitch surfaces. Pure white (`#ffffff`) is applied sparingly for primary titles and key data; high-grade slate-white (`#f8fafc`) renders primary continuous reading; secondary annotations drop down to `#94a3b8` and `#64748b` to preserve spatial hierarchy without low-contrast illegibility.

- **Dual-Font System:** **Inter** commands titles, operational actions, and body narrative. **JetBrains Mono** operates as a companion face for terminal commands, streak tallies, memory scores, timer states, and code snippets.
- **High-DPI Clarity:** Letter tracking on all headline tiers remains clamped tightly (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) to counter horizontal bleed in OLED and dark-mode rendering.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout model employs a mathematically rigid 12-column responsive fluid grid bounded by fixed structural parameters:
- **Max Widths:** Dashboard workspace constrained at `1360px`; core technical reading panes capped at `840px` to maintain optimal characters-per-line counts.
- **Rhythm & Grid:** Built entirely around a base `4px` / `8px` metric scale. Spacing tokens explicitly drive component distance, gutters, and view margins.

### Form-Factor Adaptations
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, `margin: 1rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Dynamic bottom docking panel replaces persistent rails; multi-column analysis grids fold into single-column cards.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8 columns, `margin: 1.5rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Split-screen analytical canvas with collapsible rail navigation.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 columns, `margin: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Multi-tier layout featuring persistent utility dock (`240px`), primary fluid viewport, and context metadata inspection shelf (`320px`).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth relies on structural luminance and luminous backsplashes rather than muddy drop shadows. Because dark surfaces absorb diffuse cast shadows, separation is achieved through tiered surface values, crisp hairline vector outlines, and radiant backdrops.

### Surface Tiers & Hairline Framing
- **Tier 0 (Obsidian Ground):** `#0b0f19`. Unbounded baseline.
- **Tier 1 (Base Panel):** `#111827` framed by a 1px border of `#1e293b`.
- **Tier 2 (Floating Card / Row Active):** `#1a2234` bounded by 1px `#2d3748`.
- **Tier 3 (Modals, Overlays & Popovers):** `#1f293d` with a 1px `#2d3748` edge and a deep-space ambient drop: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75), 0 4px 16px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.

### Luminous Indigo Halo
Interactive states and high-priority milestones produce a subtle radial or vector glow:
- **Interactive Focus:** `0 0 0 1px #6366f1, 0 0 16px 2px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.28)`.
- **Active Card Glow:** Top-edge specular stroke (`linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(99,102,241,0.4) 0%, rgba(129,140,248,0.1) 100%)`) providing subtle directional illumination.

## Shapes
Geometry is structured, disciplined, and ergonomic. Corners are smoothed to balance structural rigidity with sleek digital fluidity.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Inputs, buttons, segmented toggles, dropdown triggers, and interactive list items.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg` / `1rem` / 16px):** Modular cards, code inspection windows, chart containers, and modal sheets.
- **Extra Large Radius (`rounded-xl` / `1.5rem` / 24px):** Primary viewport containers, contextual overlays, and modal dialogues.
- **Pill Radius (`9999px`):** Status chips, performance tags, keyboard command badges, and counter pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#6366f1` background, `#ffffff` text, 1px `#818cf8` border with an inner highlight sheen. Hover elevates to `#4f46e5` accompanied by an indigo outer aura (`0 0 14px rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary:** Surface `#1a2234`, text `#f8fafc`, 1px border `#2d3748`. Hover triggers surface `#1f293d` and border `#374151`.
- **Ghost / Tool:** Transparent fill, text `#94a3b8`, shifting to `#111827` surface and `#f8fafc` text on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Streak & Momentum Badges:** Translucent amber fill `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`, text `#fbbf24`, 1px border `rgba(251, 191, 36, 0.25)`. Numeric counter renders in JetBrains Mono.
- **Mastery Badges:** Translucent emerald `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`, text `#34d399`, 1px border `rgba(52, 211, 153, 0.25)`.
- **Critical / Alert Badges:** Translucent crimson `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.12)`, text `#f87171`, 1px border `rgba(248, 113, 113, 0.25)`.
- **Filter Tags:** Pill geometry, `#111827` surface, `#94a3b8` text, 1px `#1e293b` border. Selected state activates `#1e1b4b` background, `#818cf8` text, and `#6366f1` outline.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 38px, `#111827` background, 1px `#2d3748` border, `#f8fafc` text, `#64748b` placeholder. Active focus triggers 1px `#6366f1` outline and a soft `rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.25)` halo.
- **Checkboxes:** 16x16px, `rounded` (4px), `#111827` surface, 1px `#2d3748` border. Active state transitions to `#6366f1` with an etched `#ffffff` check icon.
- **Radio Buttons:** 16x16px circular boundary, `#111827` background, 1px `#2d3748` border. Checked renders an inner 6px glowing `#818cf8` beacon.

### Cards & Knowledge Blocks
- Structure uses `#111827` surface with a continuous 1px `#1e293b` border and `1rem` corner radius.
- Interactive states lift to `#1a2234` surface with a `#2d3748` border and an ambient indigo rim light.

### Visual Metrics & Sparklines
- **Sparklines:** Delicately stroked (1.5px) vector paths utilizing `#818cf8` or `#34d399` over a dark-mode gradient fill tapering down to `rgba(11, 15, 25, 0)` at the bottom axis.
- **Progress Rails:** 4px tall tracks using `#1f293d` base fill, with progress fills driven by `#6366f1` (or `#34d399` for completed mastery) capped with a 1px luminous tip.
