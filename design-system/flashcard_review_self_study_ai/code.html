Question

Why does Raft require a majority quorum for leader election?

Answer MIT 6.824 Lecture 5, p. 3

Raft requires a strict majority quorum (\(\lfloor n/2 \rfloor + 1\)) to ensure that any two majorities must overlap by at least one node (the pigeonhole principle).

1

Split-brain prevention: At most one candidate can collect a majority in any given term, guaranteeing that no two leaders can be elected concurrently.

2

Leader completeness: The overlapping node in any subsequent election is guaranteed to hold the most up-to-date committed entries from previous terms.
